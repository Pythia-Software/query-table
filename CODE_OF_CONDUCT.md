# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want query-table to be a welcoming, practical, and technically rigorous
project. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- welcome questions and different experience levels;
- give credit, respect privacy, and avoid unwanted promotion;
- accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
release channels, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. If no private community
channel is listed, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting form
and begin the report title with `Community conduct`; do not include sensitive
details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence, avoid conflicts of interest, and
respond proportionally. Responses may include a private correction, a warning,
removal of content, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban. Good-
faith reporters and affected participants will not face retaliation.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard and may
remove contributions or comments that violate it.
